### Byko Buteyko

I study positioning on Hyperliquid perps. I build the data pipeline myself, then write down what the numbers show and where they stop.

**Hyperliquid whale positioning** · [write-up](https://bykobuteyko.com/research/hyperliquid-whales/) · [live dashboard](https://whales.bykobuteyko.com) · [code](https://github.com/BykoButeyko/hyperliquid-whale-positioning)
I track about 4,000 large wallets every 15 minutes and split their positions into directional bets, market making, vaults and hedged shorts. In HYPE, two thirds of the big shorts turn out to be staking hedges.

**Hyperliquid vs Binance/Bybit funding** · [write-up](https://bykobuteyko.com/research/hyperliquid-funding/) · [code](https://github.com/BykoButeyko/hyperliquid-funding-gap)
I compared three and a half years of hourly funding on BTC, ETH, SOL and HYPE. The gap averaged about 6.5% a year, shrank to 1 to 5% in 2026, and once you pay to hold both legs it earns about what T-bills pay.

**Bitcoin ETF flows and the basis trade** · [write-up](https://bykobuteyko.com/research/bitcoin-etf-basis/) · [code](https://github.com/BykoButeyko/etf-flows)
For each BTC that flowed into US spot bitcoin ETFs in 2025-26, hedge funds added 0.23 BTC of short on CME futures the same week. I fixed the hypotheses before testing, and two of my five calls failed.

**Hyperliquid liquidation cascades** · [code](https://github.com/BykoButeyko/hyperliquid-liquidation-cascades)
A websocket collector records trade flow, book depth and liquidation fills on the same four coins. I want to know how often liquidations chain into cascades and how much of the move comes back. Still collecting: I don't read a result before 30 separate cascades.
